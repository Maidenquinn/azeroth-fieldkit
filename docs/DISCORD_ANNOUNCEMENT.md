# 🧭 Azeroth FieldKit
**Combat and HUD tools for Azeroth Survivors · EN / FR**

Thanks to the team for allowing me to share FieldKit here as a verified addon!

FieldKit is my maintained fork of **MASH, originally created by Malk** — full credit to him for the original addon.

**What it offers**
• Integrated scrolling combat text: damage/healing filters, colors, fonts, grouped hits and movable areas.
• Player, target and boss health: numbers, percentage or both.
• Boss portraits independent of your selected target.
• Damage totals, DPS and contribution, plus damage-taken and lair windows.
• Collapsible windows, linked left-side cards, decorative/classic styling and background opacity.
• Gold and next-chest price above the action bar.
• Optional chest icons and guardian **lair** markers on the minimap.
• Normal/Heroic context, labelled mechanic references and an English/French switch.

**Install**
Download **AzerothFieldKit-0.2.1-review.3.zip** below, extract the inner `AzerothFieldKit` folder into your active client's `AddOns` folder, enable it and restart the game. Disable MASH to avoid overlapping UI changes.

Open **`/fieldkit`** → enable the tools you want → **Move windows** to arrange them. Some optional modules start disabled. No external executable or extra addon is required.

**Known limits:** ground runes are not tracked. Weekly affix names, marks and star progression are not supplied when unavailable from the API. The latest edge-drag fix still needs in-game feedback. The current version retains its `review.3` label.

📦 Download: https://github.com/Maidenquinn/azeroth-fieldkit/releases/tag/v0.2.1-review.3
💻 Source & bug reports: https://github.com/Maidenquinn/azeroth-fieldkit

If something breaks, include the addon/client versions, reproduction steps and any Lua error. Please hide private chat in screenshots. Thanks for the feedback!
